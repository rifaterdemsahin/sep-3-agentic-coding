🎬 About this video

The 5W1H behind "Task Decomposition" — the story this asset library exists to support.

❓ Why
To push back on the "work will be optional in 10 years" narrative with counter-evidence — history, economics, and current labor data — rather than vibes.
🤔 What
A researched rebuttal video that lays AI agent workflows's job-apocalypse claims side-by-side with the pro/con case, then argues for a more grounded, human-in-the-loop future of work.
🛠️ How
By arguing that knowledge combined with AI opens up brand-new sectors rather than draining a fixed pool of jobs — see the full case in 🧩 Arguments, built from 🔍 Research and carried into the 📝 Script → 🎨 Design → 🎞️ Previsualisation pipeline.
⏰ When
After generative AI — this is a post-ChatGPT/LLM-era argument; the job-apocalypse claims it's debunking only started circulating once generative AI went mainstream.
📍 Where
Globally — the effect of AI on jobs isn't confined to one country or industry, so the video's argument and evidence are drawn from and apply worldwide.
